About me

I am a Ph.D. student in the Artie McFerrin Department of Chemical Engineering at Texas A&M University, working with Prof. Jodie Lutkenhaus.

My research sits at the intersection of polymer science and separations. During my M.S. at Middle East Technical University (METU), I developed cellulose acetate nanofiltration membranes for separating salts from neutral organic molecules, working from polymer solution design and phase separation through to scale-up testing. Before that, I worked on polyelectrolyte multilayer membranes and on molecular dynamics simulations of Janus particles.

In the Lutkenhaus group I am excited to build on this background with polyelectrolyte complexes and functional polymeric materials.

Research

Polymeric materials & polyelectrolytes

Ph.D. · Lutkenhaus Research Group, Texas A&M · 2026 – present

Nanofiltration membranes for desalting organic solutions

M.S. Thesis · Çulfaz-Emecen Group, METU · 2023 – 2026

Cellulose acetate membranes for separating salts from ethylene glycol and other neutral organics: tuning casting solvent composition and post-treatment to improve selectivity and stability, and testing at larger scale.

Polyelectrolyte multilayer membranes

Undergraduate research · Çulfaz-Emecen Group, METU

Layer-by-layer assembly of polyelectrolytes on porous supports to create thin, selective separation layers.

Molecular dynamics of Janus particles

TÜBİTAK STAR Fellow · Fluid Frame Lab, Bilkent University

Simulating the self-assembly and dynamics of Janus particles with LAMMPS and analyzing trajectories with OVITO.

Statistical data science

Data analysis and statistical computing in R in a Unix environment.
